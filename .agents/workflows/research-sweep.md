# Workflow: /research-sweep

1. Identify the topic or integration to investigate (Kick API, Cloudflare Workers, OBS overlays, etc.).
2. Locate official primary documentation or peer-reviewed open-access literature.
3. Verify permanent free tier availability without credit card requirements.
4. Record verified entry in `research/SOURCES.md` with: ID, Title, Author, URL, Type, Access, Date, Relevance, 3-line Summary, and Confidence Rating (A/B/C).
5. Extract key architectural takeaways into corresponding skill or ADR document.
