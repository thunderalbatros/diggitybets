# Rule: Compliance & Responsible Gambling

1. All viewer participation in any game, roulette, bonus hunt, battle, or tournament MUST be 100% free of charge.
2. Absolutely NO entry fees, tips, subscriptions, or affiliate requirements as conditions for participation.
3. The platform NEVER touches, holds, or manages real money, crypto, or spectator funds.
4. Mandatory +18 age verification notices and responsible gaming banners must be present on overlays and web pages.
5. Privacy: Store only minimal viewer data (Kick ID, display name, command arguments). Enforce 90-day retention and data deletion mechanisms.
