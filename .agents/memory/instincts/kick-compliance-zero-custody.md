# Kick ToS Compliance & Zero Spectator Custody

## Action
Strictly enforce:
1. DiggityBets points ($DIG) and tournament predictions are 100% free virtual points.
2. NEVER implement deposit, buy-in, subscription requirement, or custody of real money/crypto.
3. Chatters can never buy points or pay to join bonus hunts, tournaments, or mini-games.
4. All real-world prizes are awarded out-of-band directly by streamer Tomás.
5. Include +18 and Responsible Gambling disclaimers in all public UI and overlays.

## Evidence
- Kick Terms of Service & Gambling Policy (2025/2026): Spectator-funded gambling (buy-ins, shared bankrolls) triggers immediate channel ban.
- Enforced in `AGENTS.md` Section 2.2 as an inviolable constraint.
